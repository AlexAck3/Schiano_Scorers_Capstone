RESERVED / DO NOT USE: pins 27 (GPIO0) and 28 (GPIO1) - HAT ID EEPROM
POWER: 3V3 = pins 1, 17 | 5V = pins 2, 4
GROUND: pins 6, 9, 14, 20, 25, 30, 34, 39
 
DEVICES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
TC amps (3x MAX31856), SPI0: SCLK=11, MISO=9, MOSI=10 shared; 
        CS: oven=8, top=25, bottom=24

IR sensor (MLX90614), I2C bus 1, address 0x5A: SDA=2, SCL=3

Rotary encoder: A=5, B=6, push=21 | Up button=16 | Down button=20

PWM power=19, coil select=26 (sensors.py Run_PWM only; GUI does not drive these yet)
 
CONFLICTS TO WATCH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
* sensors.py Read_data() uses D5 as a TC CS - that is the encoder A pin (GPIO5).
  Do not run Read_data() alongside the GUI, or change its CS pin.

* Don't run sensors.py (Read_SPI/Run_PWM) and gui.py at the same time: both claim
  GPIO8 and the SPI/I2C buses.
  
* Counts: 15 GPIO used, 11 GPIO free (+2 reserved).
